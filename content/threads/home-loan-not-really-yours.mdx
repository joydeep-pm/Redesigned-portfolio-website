When you take a home loan, you assume the bank that approved it will service it throughout. But the reality of modern lending is far more complex.

## The Hidden Journey of Your Loan

Your home loan might travel through multiple institutions during its lifetime:
- **Origination** - The lender who approves your loan
- **Servicing** - The entity collecting your EMIs
- **Ownership** - The actual holder of your debt

These can be three different entities.

## Why This Matters

Understanding loan ownership helps you:
1. Know who to contact for issues
2. Understand why terms might change
3. Navigate prepayment and foreclosure
4. Protect yourself during disputes

## The Co-Lending Angle

With RBI's co-lending guidelines, this complexity is increasing. Banks and NBFCs now jointly originate loans, splitting both risk and return.

For borrowers, this means:
- Multiple parties involved in your loan
- Different servicing experiences
- Complex escalation paths

## What You Can Do

1. Always get the loan agreement reviewed
2. Understand the servicing arrangement upfront
3. Keep records of all communication
4. Know your rights under RBI guidelines
