Co-lending is one of the most significant regulatory innovations in Indian lending. Here's how to build systems that work.

## Understanding Co-lending

RBI's co-lending model allows banks and NBFCs to jointly originate loans:
- **Bank share:** Minimum 20% of the loan
- **NBFC share:** Maximum 80% of the loan
- **Risk sharing:** Proportional to contribution

## Technical Architecture Considerations

### 1. Loan Origination
- Single application, dual underwriting
- Unified customer experience
- Split disbursement handling

### 2. Servicing Complexity
- Single EMI collection
- Proportional distribution
- Unified customer communication

### 3. Default Management
- NPA recognition alignment
- Collection coordination
- Recovery sharing

## Key Implementation Challenges

1. **Data synchronization** between bank and NBFC systems
2. **Reconciliation** of daily transactions
3. **Regulatory reporting** for both entities
4. **Customer service** coordination

## Our Approach at M2P

We built the co-lending orchestration module to handle:
- Real-time loan allocation
- Automated compliance checks
- Unified servicing layer
- Transparent reporting

- 5 enterprise accounts onboarded
- $50M+ loan volume processed
