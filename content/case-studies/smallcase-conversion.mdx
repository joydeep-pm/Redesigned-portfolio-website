## Funnel Analysis

Drop-off points identified:
- Signup → KYC: 40% drop (friction)
- Browse → Select: 35% drop (decision paralysis)
- Select → Invest: 25% drop (hesitation)

## Proposed Solutions

### Solution 1: Progressive KYC (+15%)
- Allow browsing without KYC
- Mini KYC (Aadhaar OTP) at first investment
- Full KYC post first transaction

### Solution 2: Risk-Based Recommendations (+12%)
- 3-question risk assessment
- Personalized top 5 recommendations
- Clear risk-return visualization

### Solution 3: First Investment Confidence (+10%)
- Micro-investment option (₹100 minimum)
- Paper portfolio simulation
- First investment cashback (₹50)

## Prioritization

| Solution | Impact | Effort | Priority |
|----------|--------|--------|----------|
| Risk-based Reco | High | Medium | P0 |
| Micro-investment | Medium | Low | P0 |
| Progressive KYC | High | High | P1 |
