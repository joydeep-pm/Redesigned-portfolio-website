## Market Opportunity

- D2C market in India: $100Bn+ at 25% CAGR
- 800+ funded D2C brands, 50,000+ bootstrapped
- Traditional banks ill-equipped for D2C financial patterns

## D2C Pain Points

1. Cash flow volatility (revenue spikes, consistent expenses)
2. Multi-channel reconciliation (Shopify, Amazon, social)
3. Working capital timing (30-60 day cycles)
4. Compliance complexity (GST, TDS, marketplace deductions)

## Product Solution

### Core Platform

1. **Smart Business Account** - Zero-balance, multi-signatory, API integration
2. **Unified Commerce Dashboard** - Shopify, Amazon integrations, auto-reconciliation
3. **Working Capital Suite** - Invoice financing, receivables financing
4. **Compliance Autopilot** - Automated GST, TDS tracking, audit-ready reports
5. **Expense Management** - Corporate cards, spend controls, vendor automation

## Business Model

- Float income from balances
- Interchange from card transactions
- Credit spread on working capital
- SaaS fees for premium features
