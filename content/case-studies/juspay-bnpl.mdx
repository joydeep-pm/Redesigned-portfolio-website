## Challenge

MoreSimpl was stagnant at 5% MoM growth. Target: accelerate to 30% MoM within 6 months while maintaining credit quality.

## Multi-Pronged Strategy

### Lever 1: Checkout Visibility (+8%)
- Dynamic placement based on cart value vs credit limit
- Clear value proposition messaging
- Trust signals (RBI regulated, secure)

### Lever 2: Smart Activation (+7%)
- Cohort-based activation sequences
- First-transaction incentives
- Merchant-funded offers

### Lever 3: Dynamic Credit Limits (+6%)
- Real-time limit refresh based on repayment
- Category-specific allocation
- Limit increase as activation incentive

### Lever 4: Category Expansion (+5%)
Priority: Groceries, Healthcare, Education, Subscriptions

### Lever 5: Personalization (+4%)
- Purchase frequency patterns
- Category preferences
- Repayment reliability score

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Baseline | Target |
|--------|----------|--------|
| MoM Growth | 5% | 30% |
| Activation Rate | 15% | 40% |
| Credit Utilization | 25% | 50% |
