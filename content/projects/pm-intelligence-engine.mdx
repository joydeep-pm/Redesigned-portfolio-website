## 🔍 The Problem

Product managers consume hours of podcast content but lack a personalized way to find episodes that match their working style and philosophy. With **295 episodes** of Lenny's Podcast, finding the right insights for your specific challenges is like searching for a needle in a haystack.

## 💡 The Solution

An interactive experience where PMs discover their philosophy through a **10-question quiz** built from real podcast transcripts. Every insight, quote, and recommendation is grounded in actual data—no generic advice.

## 🛠️ Technical Implementation

### 🧠 Philosophy Mapping System
- 8 distinct PM archetypes identified through content analysis
- 4 core dimensions: Velocity vs Perfection, Data vs Intuition, Discovery vs Focus, Alignment vs Chaos
- Algorithm matches quiz responses to philosophy zones

### 📝 AI-Powered Quote Curation
- Claude Code extracted and verified 1,200+ quotes from transcripts
- Each quote tagged with exact transcript line numbers for verification
- Semantic matching to philosophy dimensions

### 🎯 Recommendation Engine
- 5 personalized episodes that match your philosophy
- 3 contrarian episodes to challenge your worldview
- Smart matching based on verified quote database

### 🌌 Immersive 3D Experience
- Three.js starfield visualization with React Three Fiber
- Dark sci-fi aesthetic with terminal-style typography
- Framer Motion animations throughout
