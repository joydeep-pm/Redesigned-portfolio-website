Joined as Senior Product Manager to build a BNPL product for Aramex, the largest logistics network in MENA. From December 2021, Lead Product Manager for the Finvolv LMS across 12+ financial institutions.

## Aramex SMART (BNPL, MENA)

About 86% of e-commerce orders in the region were cash on delivery. Aramex SMART created the loan on delivery rather than at checkout, so it mirrored the cash-on-delivery habit while adding credit. As sole product owner, led it from inception to go-live in 6 months, through a full COVID-19 re-plan.

- **Scale:** 500K+ users across Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Nigeria, with a 5mn SAR live portfolio
- **Underwriting:** A real-time engine built on 20+ alternate parameters from Aramex delivery history, since bureau data was sparse. It lifted approvals by 45%
- **Four products:** Pay Later (14-day), Pay in Installments (3x) and Prepaid shipped; Part Pay piloted in the UAE
- **COVID pivot:** The planned launch relied on physical events. Re-planned it around essential-goods e-tailers and digital onboarding, and went live in June 2020 on schedule
- **Merchant growth:** 2 merchants at launch, 25 within three months, 35+ by the end. Magento, Shopify and WooCommerce plug-ins plus a self-service Merchant Portal removed the engineering bottleneck
- **Payments:** PayPal, STC Pay, Telr and Apple Pay integrations; Arabic localisation

## Finvolv LMS (B2B SaaS)

- **API-first stack:** 83% reduction in time-to-market for new loan products
- **Revenue:** 3x ARR growth, with 27% QoQ growth
- **Integrations:** 5 LMS integrations averaging 3 weeks each
- **Planning:** Introduced quarterly OKR planning and structured client onboarding documentation
