Owned the loan management system behind Paytm Postpaid, India's largest BNPL product, across Postpaid, Personal Loan and Merchant Loan. Managed three product managers covering LMS core, subscriptions and TechOps.

## Key Achievements

- **Scale:** 10.1M active loans at peak, ₹5,200+ Cr (USD 626Mn) in total loan volume, 4x YoY disbursal growth and 134% YoY loan count growth
- **Subscriptions BNPL:** Let Postpaid pay for recurring spend such as OTT, utilities and insurance. It reached ₹15Cr monthly GMV across 7 internal systems, with a statement of work covering 8 use cases and their refund and accounting flows
- **UPI Autopay:** Standing repayment mandates cut churn by 10% and repayment-related support queries by 4%
- **Lender platform:** 5 lending partners integrated with automated settlement and daily reconciliation
- **Revenue data product:** Replaced a manual billing process across 10M+ loans and cut billing cycle time by 65%
- **TechOps automation:** 40% fewer Postpaid support queries. Excess-payment reversals (20,000+ a month) and refunds (30,000+ a month) now run without an agent
- **Compliance:** Kept the platform current with RBI digital lending guidelines, NACH rules, data localisation and credit bureau reporting
