Leading product vision and delivery for the Core Lending Suite on Finflux by M2P: an API-first, modular lending platform for banks, NBFCs and fintechs, covering origination, loan management and collections. Joined as Director in Nov 2024; promoted to Senior Director in Jul 2026.

## Key Achievements

- **Co-lending:** Multi-lender module with three simultaneous repayment schedules (customer, NBFC and bank), escrow-routed cashflows and real-time reconciliation. Live for 5 enterprise accounts with $50M+ loan volume, 90% less reconciliation effort and payout generation down from 3 days to 3 hours
- **RBI Co-Lending Directions 2025:** Authored "Decoding Co-Lending 2025", mapping each clause to a product capability, then shipped the compliance changes: blended-rate recast, escrow, NPA sync between lenders and dual-bureau reporting
- **Loans against securities:** Mutual funds and shares, with digital pledging through depositories and registrars, an LTV engine, daily shortfall management and Account Aggregator origination. Supports a $200M+ customer portfolio and a 25% platform revenue increase
- **Gold loan:** Digital appraisal, LTV by purity, and fraud-prevention controls (appraiser verification, geo-tagged photos, CERSAI registration and duplicate-pledge detection)
- **Agentic LOS (in development):** AI agents for document collection, verification, credit data, underwriting and offers, with every action logged and exceptions routed to humans
- **Workflow automation:** 60% less operational overhead through configurable billing, moratorium and DPD/NPA workflows
- **Other verticals:** LAP, term loans, supply chain finance, education, auto, consumer durables and overdraft, each configured on the platform
